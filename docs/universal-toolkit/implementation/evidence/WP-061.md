# WP-061 — Calibrated audio controls and meters

**State: active; package not accepted.** WP-060 real audio engine remains open.

Sol added `AudioMeterModel` and `measureInterleaved` as the first measurement
slice. A known two-channel block checks peak/RMS dBFS, overload latch, hold,
fall, silence and nonfinite input. On the merged Qt 6.11 tree, the targeted
`tst_audio_meter_model` build and CTest passed 1/1 using configured
`-j24 -l24` build limits. The model exposes compact per-channel GUI snapshots;
its interleaved fixture path is explicitly non-realtime.

Sol also added `AudioParameterMap` for linear, logarithmic and dB gain
descriptors, with explicit silence at position zero and exact 0 dB unity
position. Knob/Fader can share this provider mapping for pointer and keyboard
travel while leaving the edit transaction with the host. Its targeted Qt 6.11
build and `tst_audio_parameter_map` CTest passed 1/1 using `-j24 -l24`.

`Knob`, `Fader`, `AudioMeter`, and `LoudnessHistory` with mapper hooks,
transactional gesture requests, channel meter values, and provider-owned
history. Its configured-limit `-j24 -l24` build, focused QML test (4/4)
and preview check passed. Sol inspected the [audio controls screenshot](WP-061-audio-controls.png): meter tracks, clip reset, gain/pan travel and history
plot are visible. The fixture uses synthetic provider snapshots.
On the merged tree, `example_audio-controls` passed its focused CTest smoke.

## Code-first round (`work/wp061-cf`, on `round/cf1`): engine binding

Written and syntax-checked without building. Every test below is new or
extended and has not run yet; the round build runs it. Design:
[audio-measurement.md](../../../audio-measurement.md), decisions D-100 to
D-103 in [decisions.md](../../../decisions.md).

1. **Parameter taper/units, touch/automation state, channel layouts,
   quantities and windows.** `AudioParameterBinding` publishes the engine
   parameter's unit, range, step, taper (`gainDb`, `logarithmic` or `linear`,
   through an owned `AudioParameterMap`), silence floor, static and reset
   values, automation mode and state (`touch, writing`, `read, held`, …).
   `AudioMeterModel` names channels from the stream layout (`M`; `L`, `R`)
   and declares its metric, 10 ms block, 300 ms RMS window, hold and fall in
   `integrationWindow`. `AudioLoudnessModel` declares ITU-R BS.1770-4
   momentary (400 ms), short-term (3 s) and gated integrated values, the
   channel layout and weights, and the history metric and window. Tests:
   `tst_audio_parameter_binding::describesTheEngineParameter`,
   `tst_audio_meter_tap::tapMeasuresKnownTonesInTenMillisecondBlocks`,
   `tst_audio_loudness::historyResetAndReport`.
2. **One parameter transaction per gesture, with double-click and reset
   semantics.** `Knob` and `Fader` take `binding` and route pointer (relative
   drag, no jump on press), wheel bursts (ended by the new theme role
   `Tk.Theme.motion.gestureIdle`, kept under reduced motion), held arrow
   keys, Home/End, numeric entry and resets through `begin` → `preview`* →
   `commit`/`cancel`. A double-click reset joins the open pointer gesture, so
   it is one undo step, and an unchanged click stores nothing. Commits are an
   automation pass (`recorded`), a static-value edit (`static`, one
   `AutomationModel` history step) or live-only in read mode over a curve
   (`live`). While a control is held, the engine plays its value in every mode
   (D-100: `EngineScheduleSnapshot` overrides on `touched`, and mode `off`
   plays `defaultValue`). `AudioSession` forwards the engine transport to the
   automation clock, and `AutomationModel::discardPass` drops a canceled pass.
   A NumberField typed edit no longer detaches the field from the control's
   value. Tests: `tst_audio_parameter_binding` (static, cancel, unchanged,
   joined begin, read, off, touch, latch, destroyed binding),
   `tst_audio_controls.qml::test_bound_fader_gestures_are_engine_transactions`
   (keyboard step, Escape-canceled drag, double-click reset = one
   transaction, undo), `::test_bound_knob_wheel_burst_is_one_transaction`,
   and the two earlier control tests, unchanged.
3. **Meter and loudness computation in the audio layer, bounded GUI
   snapshots, hold/decay/clip reset.** `AudioMeterTap` runs in
   `AudioEngineProcessor::process` after the graph renders. It measures peak,
   squares and BS.1770 K-weighted squares per channel in 10 ms blocks and
   hands them to the GUI through a preallocated 1024-block SPSC ring (full:
   drop and count, never wait). `AudioEngine.outputMeter` and
   `outputLoudness` drain it on every poll. Ballistics run on each block's
   audio duration (D-102). Loudness gating uses a bounded 0.01 LU histogram
   with exact energy sums (D-103). `resetClip()` clears latches, maxima and
   invalid counts; `AudioLoudnessModel::reset()` clears the measurement and
   history. Tests: `tst_audio_meter_tap`, `tst_audio_loudness`, and
   `tst_audio_engine_meter` (live `builtin:null` device). The existing
   `tst_audio_engine` allocation probe now also covers the tap, because every
   stereo period is measured.
4. **Readable calibrated scales, overload state, accessible alternatives.**
   The Fader's scale and unity marks follow the mapper under the thumb
   centre, and the silence floor reads −∞. The previous marks were linear,
   so a mapped fader's marks disagreed with its thumb. `AudioMeter` has a
   labelled dBFS scale row, channel names, a held-peak readout, a warning
   zone at `warningDbfs`, the clip latch and **Reset clip** into the model,
   and a per-channel accessible summary (peak, RMS, hold, max, clip, metric,
   window). `LoudnessHistory` has a labelled level scale, an M/S/I readout,
   a time-based plot, reset into the model and `reportExported(report)`
   (`qindatk.loudness-report/1` with provenance). Tests:
   `tst_audio_controls.qml::test_meter_view_projects_a_measured_snapshot`,
   `::test_history_view_shows_bs1770_readings`. Scope: screen-reader runs
   belong to WP-088…WP-094.
5. **Verification: known signals, silence and non-finite input, channel
   changes, ballistics timing, undo and automation.** Known-amplitude tones
   give exact peaks, RMS within 1e-5 dB, and loudness within 0.001 LU of the
   analytic K-filter level (EBU Tech 3341 cases 1–2). EBU cases 3 and 5 are
   within ±0.1 LU, and the absolute gate, digital silence and NaN/inf samples
   are checked. A channel change resets both the model and the live engine
   (Stereo → Mono). Hold (1490 ms held, 1500 ms falls) and fall give
   identical snapshots at 10 ms and 70 ms publish cadences. Undo and redo of
   static edits and automation passes are checked through the rendered graph
   (the device's sample reference) and heard in the live meters. D-101: a dB
   gain's range minimum renders exact zeros. Scope: performance
   measurement, other platforms and output-fidelity comparison belong to
   WP-088…WP-094.

**Accept when** (not yet verified; round build pending): meters report defined
audio quantities against known signals (`tst_audio_meter_tap`,
`tst_audio_loudness`, `tst_audio_engine_meter`), and controls manipulate
actual engine parameters consistently (`tst_audio_parameter_binding`,
`tst_audio_engine_meter`, `tst_audio_controls.qml`).

The example `examples/audio-controls/Main.qml` now binds its Knob and Fader
to one session gain parameter, binds the frequency Knob to a logarithmic
parameter, and measures generated test tones instead of drawing synthetic
snapshots. Its screenshot above predates this change and needs a new grab.

Scope notes: per-track and bus meters and channel strips on a mixer graph
belong to WP-062 (the tap is the reusable measurement point). True peak,
loudness range and certified loudness compliance are not claimed. Input
metering is not part of this package.
