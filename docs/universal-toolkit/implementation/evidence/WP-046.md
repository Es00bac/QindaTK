# WP-046 — Exact time and tempo conversions

**State: accepted.** Proposal scope: AUD-01 TimeMap. The public API, numeric
range and rounding policy are in [exact time and tempo map](../../../time-map.md)
and [D-036](../../../decisions-services.md#d-036--exact-time-storage-with-decimal-qml-transfer).

## Behavior delivered

- `TimeMap` stores signed 64-bit sample positions, frame indices and musical
  ticks. A portable four-limb 128-bit intermediate preserves exact integer
  arithmetic across high-rate and long-duration conversions without adding a
  Boost dependency or requiring GNU `__int128`.
- Rational frame rate and piecewise constant integer-microsecond tempo
  segments convert to and from sample time with explicit quantization. Tempo
  boundaries are continuous; the first tempo extends to negative ticks.
- QML-facing positions, origin and results are decimal strings. Tempo segment
  ticks also require decimal strings, avoiding precision loss through QML
  `Number`. Relative sample-to-pixel transforms reject spans beyond the exact
  display range. Format/parse supports non-drop timecode and drop-frame labels
  at 30,000/1,001 and 60,000/1,001, including skipped-label validation.

## Verification

- The focused `tst_time_map` suite covers positive and negative rounding,
  tempo changes, exact long positions, 2⁵³ QML boundary behavior, drop-frame
  one-minute/ten-minute/hour boundaries, negative and signed-64-bit extrema,
  invalid settings and overflow. The root extension serializes a frame,
  sample, tick and tempo map through JSON as decimal strings and verifies
  conversion after reopening.
- The integrated Qt 6.11.1 build passed full CTest **53/53** after the
  decimal-string tempo tick and JSON roundtrip checks. Qt 6.8.3 rebuilt with
  the same precision and portable-arithmetic revision; `tst_time_map` passed
  in a **54/55** expanded run whose sole failure was an in-progress WP-020
  QML test. All direct builds used the actual configured `-j24 -l24` limits.
- No layout or rendering changes are claimed for this numeric service, so
  geometry and screenshot gates do not apply. The installed consumer includes
  `Tk.TimeMap` and its public header. `docs/catalog.json` includes the type.

## Boundaries

The current environment proves Linux Qt 6.8.3 and 6.11.1 behavior. Actual
Windows/MSVC and macOS compilation, frame-accurate media-engine connection
and playback are separate platform/domain qualification. TimeMap supplies
arithmetic and presentation conversion, not an audio or video clock. Tempo
ramps, exotic drop-frame rates and external timecode offsets are unsupported
until a domain adapter defines their semantics.
