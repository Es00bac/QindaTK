# WP-045 — Raster, masks, brushes and comparison

**State: active; the finishing lane below is ready for acceptance. Sol's
integration checks and acceptance are pending.**

The `CompareView` control and `ImageCompareItem` render two equal-size images
with split, blink and absolute encoded-sRGB difference modes. They share a
single zoom and pan transform. Images with mismatched dimensions, failed
loads or more than 16 million pixels are rejected. The control accepts local
image URLs; hosts can also pass `QImage` values to the C++ renderer.

The configured-limit integration build passed. `tst_image_compare_item`
passed with pixel checks for all three modes and a mismatched-image case.
The [1100×760 fixture screenshot](WP-045-image-compare.png) was opened and
inspected. Its dump places `CompareView#compareFixture` at `8,75 1084x677`,
with `ImageCompareItem#compareImage` at `0,0 1068x596` inside its viewport.

The item now also reads bounded PNG data URLs, allowing a live
`TiledRasterDocument.imageDataUrl` to drive a comparison view. The focused
comparison test passed for a pair of live data URLs.
The QML control now owns split-divider drag, blink press/release, wheel zoom,
and middle-button pan gestures. Its focused QML file passed 3/3 checks in the
merged tree.

`TiledRasterDocument` now paints premultiplied RGBA across 256 px tiles with
stable layer IDs, sparse 8-bit masks, deterministic pressure/tilt/hardness
sampling, one transaction per stroke, bounded undo/redo and atomic composite
PNG export. The merged configured-limit build and focused raster test passed.
The [1080×680 raster screenshot](WP-045-tiled-raster.png) was opened and
inspected: a stroke crosses a tile seam and a mask removes its center.

`MaskChannelPanel`, `BrushPresetView` and `BrushDynamicsEditor` now expose
stable host snapshots and explicit request/acknowledgment contracts. The
focused QML file passed 5/5 checks. Its
[1180×760 controls screenshot](WP-045-mask-brush-controls.png) was opened and
inspected. The [1200×850 combined workbench screenshot](WP-045-raster-workbench.png)
was also opened and inspected: the actual raster pixels and synchronized
comparison are visible alongside live mask and brush controls. The combined
fixture sends mouse strokes to the tiled document and maps control requests to
its supported actions.

The raster document now saves and reopens a versioned atomic state containing
tile pixels, sparse mask coverage, stable layer IDs and the brush preset. Its
composite image and PNG output carry an sRGB tag. The merged configured-limit
build and focused raster test passed. The
[1080×680 persistence screenshot](WP-045-raster-persistence.png) was opened and
inspected; the dump shows the `Save State` and `Reopen` actions and a painted,
masked stroke with the saved document status visible.

`RasterStrokeInput` now maps target-local tablet or mouse coordinates into
raster pixels and sends pressure and tilt samples to the document. One gesture
commits one stroke; Escape, lost grab, disable, or context replacement cancels
the in-flight stroke. The merged configured-limit build and focused synthetic
tablet/mouse/Escape test passed. The
[1080×680 input screenshot](WP-045-raster-stroke-input.png) was opened and
inspected; the dump places `RasterStrokeInput#rasterStrokeInput` at
`0,0 640x320` over the painted canvas. Actual tablet fidelity depends on the
Qt platform plugin and device driver; eraser input currently uses the
configured paint color.

At that point the open items were CanvasDocument/CanvasSurface session
integration, broader color-management work beyond the current sRGB tag, and
combined graphics qualification. The finishing lane below addresses them.

## Finishing lane — 2026-09-24 (`work/wp045-finish`)

Base: integration head `173ca40`. Environment: Qt 6.11.1, GCC 15.3.0,
`QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen`, `QT_QUICK_BACKEND=software`. The build ran in
`build/wp045` (Debug, Ninja, ccache) with
`cmake --build build/wp045 --parallel 4 --target <targets>`.

**Canvas placement (D-085).** `TiledRasterDocument.placeOnCanvas(canvas, x, y)`
adds the flattened composite and its `Image` object to a `CanvasDocument` in
one canvas edit, which is one undo step. `publishToCanvas(canvas, objectId)`
replaces that image in one edit. Every unchanged tile keeps its shared
`QImage`, and identical pixels make no edit. `canvasToRaster` maps document
points through the object's inverse transform, and
`RasterStrokeInput.pointMapper` uses that mapping to paint on a moved, scaled
or rotated placement through a `CanvasView`. Canvas transforms, vector paint
order, undo, canvas files and sessions, and ICC output now apply to raster
pixels. The raster keeps its layered state, masks, stroke history and file.
The new code is in `src/core/tiled_raster_document_output.cpp`.

**History and hidden data (CAN-10).** Raster undo now has three step kinds:
stroke tiles, mask enable toggles and saved-preset changes. A stroke into a
hidden layer or a disabled mask is refused, and the paint target is always
the explicit `paintMask` argument.

**Presets and previews (CAN-15/16).** `beginStroke` captures its preset. A
preset switch during a stroke, in the document or in `RasterStrokeInput`
(which no longer cancels on a `brushPreset` change), applies to the next
stroke only. `brushPreview`/`brushPreviewDataUrl` render a fixed pressure ramp
through the real brush engine. `BrushDynamicsEditor.previewSource` shows that
sample. `BrushPresetView` requests a preview for each created row, cancels it
when the row leaves the view (no cache buffer) or the view closes, and
`providePreview` refuses canceled IDs.

**Comparison (CAN-23).** `ImageCompareItem`/`CompareView` accept sources of the
same region at different resolutions. Both are drawn into one target, and the
difference is evaluated at the finer resolution. With `revisionKey` set,
`setSourcePair`/`setImagePair` replace both sources atomically only for that
key. A stale delivery changes nothing, and `stale`/`displayedRevisionKey`
report the shown pair.

### Scope notes

- Linear-light compositing, precision above 8 bits per channel, non-sRGB-tagged
  raster sources, other rendering intents and soft proofing are not claimed.
  Requirement 2 asks for color-aware output, which the sRGB tag and the WP-043
  canvas ICC path provide. WP-043 already leaves intents and proofing
  unsupported.
- The raster and canvas histories stay separate, and the canvas shows a stroke
  when it is published (normally at stroke end); see D-085. A shared
  raster/canvas history and live canvas feedback during a stroke are not
  WP-045 requirements.
- The canvas file stores the flattened placement. Layered raster state is
  saved in the raster file. A combined container is not required by WP-045.
- ENG-03 pixels were proven on the software provider. Scene-graph image-tile
  rendering is WP-040's accepted path, and the dual-Qt/RHI and installed
  consumer gates are the integrator's checkpoint.
- Tablet fidelity, eraser mapping, touch painting and calibration remain
  platform/device limits (see above).
- D-085 is provisional: its number was free on `173ca40`, but other lanes may
  use it too.
