# WP-064 — MIDI, patterns and clip launching

**State: active; package not accepted.**

Luna's `work/wp064-midi-editors` lane was integrated as `936320e`. It adds
`PianoRoll`, `MidiControllerLane`, `StepSequencer` and `ClipLauncher` with
stable pattern/note/event IDs and exact decimal tick strings. The controls
draw host snapshots and emit editing, launch and stop requests. Launch
acknowledgments do not fabricate playing state; the host publishes that state.

The merged Qt 6.11 focused MIDI interaction test passed, including tick
precision beyond JavaScript's safe integer range, pattern identity, and
launch acknowledgment. Preview dump geometry placed the piano roll at
1264×260, controller lane at 1264×128, sequencer at 1264×218 and launcher at
1264×148. I opened and inspected the [merged MIDI fixture](WP-064-midi-editors.png).

MIDI storage, controller encoding, scheduling/audition, quantization and
overlap policy, deterministic probability, undo, and session/MIDI interchange
remain backend work. The fixture does not claim playback.

## Code-first round cf1: music backend (work/wp064-cf)

Built on the real engine, session, mixer and tempo map; documented in
[audio-midi.md](../../../audio-midi.md), decisions D-150 to D-153. Tests below
are written, not yet run (the round build runs them).

1. **IDs, ticks, channels, velocity, expression, seed policy** — built.
   `MidiSequenceModel` (`session.midi`): UUIDs for patterns, notes, events,
   rows, steps, scenes and slots; musical ticks at the session PPQ; channels
   0–15; 16-bit velocity; 32-bit controller values with 7/14-bit MIDI 1.0
   scaling (min-centre-max); per-note pitch bend and pressure attached to
   their note; per-pattern seed with the D-151 decision function.
   Tests: `tst_midi_model` (resolutions, attached expression, JSON).
2. **Scheduling, audition, all-notes-off, import/export** — built.
   `AudioRenderGraph` compiles a `MidiClock` and pattern programs and
   renders MIDI tracks through the built-in stateless test instrument;
   `midiEvents` gives the scheduled stream (controllers included) for
   WP-066 instruments. Audition voices on the device thread with note-off,
   all-notes-off, and release on transport stop, device stop/loss and
   session detach. Standard MIDI File export (format 1, tempo/meter) and
   import (formats 0/1) with reported reductions. Tests: `tst_midi_schedule`,
   `tst_midi_engine`, `tst_midi_model` (SMF). Scope: MIDI device I/O and MIDI
   recording are not declared (`capabilities.midiDevices`); plugin
   instruments are WP-066.
3. **Insert/move/resize/quantize and lane editing, exact and reversible** —
   built. Each operation is one history step holding the complete before
   and after content; drag gestures preview and commit once; the controls'
   requests map one-to-one. Tests: `tst_midi_model`, `tst_midi_session.qml`.
4. **Polymetric patterns, quantized launching, stop/cancel** — built. Rows
   loop on their own lengths; `session.launcher` posts launches that the
   device thread quantizes (none/beat/bar) from its own clock, answers once,
   starts at the exact sample (period split), cancels replaced requests and
   stops on the grid. Tests: `tst_midi_schedule` (3 against 4, launch
   rendering and cut), `tst_midi_engine` (delayed UI, stop, cancel).
5. **Verification list** — overlapping notes, expression precision, tempo
   changes, deterministic patterns, launch boundaries and no stuck notes on
   close/device loss are each covered by the tests above (not yet run).
   Scope: performance measurement, other platforms and screen-reader runs
   are WP-088…WP-094.

**Accept when:** real events at exact positions (`tst_midi_schedule`
compares event samples and the first rendered sample, including after a
tempo change); data preserved through undo, session save/reopen and MIDI
file roundtrips (`tst_midi_model`, `tst_midi_schedule`).
