# WP-064 — MIDI, patterns and clip launching

**State: accepted after integration.**

Luna's `work/wp064-midi-editors` lane was integrated as `936320e`. It adds
`PianoRoll`, `MidiControllerLane`, `StepSequencer` and `ClipLauncher` with
stable pattern/note/event IDs and exact decimal tick strings. The controls
draw host snapshots and emit editing, launch and stop requests. Launch
acknowledgments do not fabricate playing state; the host publishes that state.

The merged Qt 6.11 focused MIDI interaction test passed, including tick
precision beyond JavaScript's safe integer range, pattern identity, and
launch acknowledgment. Preview dump geometry placed the piano roll at
1264×260, controller lane at 1264×128, sequencer at 1264×218 and launcher at
1264×148. I opened and inspected the [merged MIDI fixture](WP-064-midi-editors.png).

MIDI storage, controller encoding, scheduling/audition, quantization and
overlap policy, deterministic probability, undo, and session/MIDI interchange
remain backend work. The fixture does not claim playback.

## Code-first round cf1: music backend (work/wp064-cf)

Built on the real engine, session, mixer and tempo map; documented in
[audio-midi.md](../../../audio-midi.md), decisions D-150 to D-153. The tests
below were run in the audit-gap round (see the end of this file).

1. **IDs, ticks, channels, velocity, expression, seed policy** — built.
   `MidiSequenceModel` (`session.midi`): UUIDs for patterns, notes, events,
   rows, steps, scenes and slots; musical ticks at the session PPQ; channels
   0–15; 16-bit velocity; 32-bit controller values with 7/14-bit MIDI 1.0
   scaling (min-centre-max); per-note pitch bend and pressure attached to
   their note; per-pattern seed with the D-151 decision function.
   Tests: `tst_midi_model` (resolutions, attached expression, JSON).
2. **Scheduling, audition, all-notes-off, import/export** — built.
   `AudioRenderGraph` compiles a `MidiClock` and pattern programs and
   renders MIDI tracks through the built-in stateless test instrument,
   which plays notes and the declared controllers (CC7 volume, CC11
   expression, channel pitch bend, per-note pitch bend); `midiEvents` gives
   the full scheduled stream. Audition voices on the device thread with
   note-off, all-notes-off, and release on transport stop, device stop/loss,
   session detach and session destruction. MIDI note input from an ALSA
   sequencer port (`session.midiInput`) and engine-owned MIDI recording into
   launcher slots. Standard MIDI File export (format 1, tempo/meter) and
   import (formats 0/1) with reported reductions. Tests: `tst_midi_schedule`,
   `tst_midi_engine`, `tst_midi_launch`, `tst_midi_model` (SMF). Scope: MIDI
   output ports are not declared; no planned package hosts plugin
   instruments (WP-066 items 1–5 have no MIDI event input).
3. **Insert/move/resize/quantize and lane editing, exact and reversible** —
   built. Each operation is one history step holding the complete before
   and after content; drag gestures preview and commit once; the controls'
   requests map one-to-one. Tests: `tst_midi_model`, `tst_midi_session.qml`.
4. **Polymetric patterns, quantized launching, stop/cancel** — built. Rows
   loop on their own lengths; `session.launcher` posts launches that the
   device thread quantizes (none/beat/bar) from its own clock, answers once,
   starts at the exact sample (period split), cancels replaced requests and
   stops on the grid. Tests: `tst_midi_schedule` (3 against 4, launch
   rendering and cut), `tst_midi_engine` (delayed UI, stop, cancel).
5. **Verification list** — overlapping notes, expression precision, tempo
   changes, deterministic patterns, launch boundaries and no stuck notes on
   close/device loss are each covered by the tests above.
   Scope: performance measurement, other platforms and screen-reader runs
   are WP-088…WP-094.

**Accept when:** real events at exact positions (`tst_midi_schedule`
compares event samples and the first rendered sample, including after a
tempo change); data preserved through undo, session save/reopen and MIDI
file roundtrips (`tst_midi_model`, `tst_midi_schedule`).

## Audit-gap round (work/wp064-cf, after merging round/cf1)

An independent audit listed nine gaps. I checked each against
[phase-05-audio.md#wp-064](../phase-05-audio.md#wp-064) and the code, and all
nine were real. Mutation check: I reverted each fix in turn (loop and seek
re-base, session-destructor reset, played-duration cap, controller
shaping, pattern-use guard, PPQ rescale), rebuilt, and each matching test
failed.

1. **Launches ignored transport loop and seek (req. 4).** A transport jump
   now re-bases launcher state on the device thread
   (`AudioEngineProcessor::rebaseMidi`, D-153 amended). A playing clip keeps
   its musical progress. A waiting launch or stop is re-quantized from the
   new position and reported again. `tst_midi_launch`: launch and stop at the
   loop end fire at sample 0 after the wrap, a clip follows a backward seek,
   and a take keeps counting through a wrap.
2. **Destroyed session left clips playing (req. 5, D-153).** `~AudioSession`
   now sends `AudioEngine::releaseMidi()`: one uncounted reset that ends
   launches, audition notes and MIDI takes (`setEngine(nullptr)` uses it
   too). `tst_midi_engine destroyingASessionEndsItsClips`.
3. **Controllers never played (req. 2, ENG-10), and the scope note was
   wrong.** The test instrument now plays CC7 volume, CC11 expression,
   channel pitch bend (±2 semitones) and per-note pitch bend (±48). Pitch
   bends keep continuous phase, and rendering stays stateless and
   block-independent (D-152 amended). `tst_midi_schedule
   controllersShapeTheTestInstrument` covers the exact level scale, the
   frequency ratio from zero crossings, the absence of a phase jump, and
   block and seek equality. The note that pointed to WP-066 is corrected:
   WP-066 has no MIDI event input.
4. **No MIDI device input or recording (req. 2, AUD-25 record state).**
   `MidiInputPort` (`session.midiInput`, an ALSA sequencer input port on
   Linux) sends notes to the engine as audition notes. `session.launcher.record`
   / `stopRecording` run an engine-owned take: the device thread quantizes
   the start, stamps each note with a take-relative tick for the device
   period that applies it, and sizes the take on stop. The notes become a
   new pattern in the slot as one undo step. The slot state is `recording`,
   and ClipLauncher has `requestRecord`. `capabilities.midiDevices` now
   declares input: true and output: false. Device *configuration* stays
   with WP-060 (PLT-19). MIDI output is not declared. Tests:
   `tst_midi_launch recordingStampsNotesFromTheEngineClock`, `tst_midi_engine
   aRecordedTakeBecomesThePatternOfItsSlot` and
   `theMidiInputPortPlaysAndRecordsFromASequencerClient`, which uses a real
   ALSA client.
5. **Device-thread work grew with playback time.** A played note lasts at
   most its pattern length, since a longer note would overlap its own
   repeat. The stored duration is kept. Note and step look-back are
   separate, and each pass starts at a binary-searched note. `tst_midi_schedule
   longNotesKeepTheLookBackBounded` visits at most 3 notes 100,000 passes
   after the origin; before the fix it visited more than 100,000.
6. **removePattern and undo could orphan timeline clips.** The session
   reports which patterns its clips place (`MidiSequenceModel::setPatternUse`).
   A commit, undo or redo that would drop one of those patterns is refused
   and changes nothing. `tst_midi_schedule patternsThatClipsPlaceCannotBeRemoved`.
7. **A PPQ change moved every note.** A PPQ change now rescales the content
   and every history step exactly. The session installs this as a new
   `TimeMap` PPQ guard, which refuses a change that cannot be made exactly.
   `tst_midi_schedule ppqChangesRescaleExactlyOrAreRefused`.
8. **The SMF overlap report was O(n²).** `MidiFile::nestedNotes` is
   O(n log n) with a sparse table. It matches the pairwise definition on
   random data, and 65,536 notes take well under a second (`tst_midi_model
   nestedNoteReportIsExactAndLinear`).
9. **Untested paths.** New tests cover drags of a CC and a per-note event
   (the per-note event is re-clamped inside its note), cancel and
   `deleteEvents` (`tst_midi_model`). `tst_midi_session.qml
   test_controllerLaneEditsTheSessionModel` wires MidiControllerLane to
   `session.midi`. An explicit cancel of a queued launch is tested with the
   engine events (`tst_midi_launch`) and with the "canceled" and "Nothing
   was playing" acknowledgments (`tst_midi_engine`).

**Run (build/cf, offscreen, software):** `tst_midi_launch` 6/6,
`tst_midi_model` 12/12, `tst_midi_schedule` 11/11, `tst_midi_engine` 10/10
(ALSA test not skipped), `tst_qml -input tst_midi_session.qml` 6/6,
`tst_midi_editors.qml` 3/3. Neighbouring tests passed: `tst_audio_session`,
`tst_audio_engine`, `tst_audio_mixer_engine`, `tst_audio_take_comp`,
`tst_audio_control_surface`, `tst_time_map`, `tst_tempo_automation`,
`tst_timeline_model`, `tst_timeline_transport` and `tst_score_interchange`.
`tst_score_model tiesAccidentalsAndSpelling` fails in score code, which
this change does not touch (WP-067). No control changed its drawing, so
there are no new screenshots.

**Still open:** MIDI output ports. Hosting plugin instruments, which no
planned package covers. A timeline undo that restores a clip whose pattern
was removed later leaves that clip silent; the MIDI undo can restore the
pattern. Recording is accurate to one device period. Qualification is
deferred to WP-089…WP-094.
