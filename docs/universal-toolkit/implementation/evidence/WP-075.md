# WP-075 — Tracking and rotoscoping

**State: active; package not accepted.**

`TrackingRotoModel` tracks tagged frame features with explicit confidence and
loss, cancellable preview and acceptance, corrections, undo and persistence.
It stores stable-ID keyframed mask paths, interpolates matching topology,
propagates with accepted tracks, and applies feathered masks to real pixels.
`MotionFrameRenderer` consumes a referenced mask before the layer transform.

`TrackingPanel` and `RotoscopeEditor` present host snapshots and emit stable-ID
requests. Feature corrections use source pixel coordinates; mask vertices use
normalized source coordinates. The controls show loss rather than silently
applying a failed track, and require explicit acceptance of a propagated mask.
The [1120×720 screenshot](WP-075-tracking-roto.png) was opened and inspected:
the lost state, preview path and point handles are legible. The UI lane dump
reports `TrackingPanel` 456×654 and `RotoscopeEditor` 632×654.

The isolated model test passed 1/1, the QML control case passed 3/3, and the
fixture passed `qtk-preview --check`. After integration, the configured-limit
merged build and `tst_tracking_roto_model` passed 1/1, the focused QML control
case passed 3/3, and the fixture passed `qtk-preview --check`. Sol recaptured
and opened the merged screenshot; its dump reports `TrackingPanel` 456×654,
`RotoscopeEditor` 632×654, and `rotoFrameSurface` 616×347.

The [live 1120×720 screenshot](WP-075-tracking-roto-live.png) was recaptured
and opened after wiring the controls to `TrackingRotoModel`. Three local SVG
frames move a textured feature by 6 and 12 source pixels. The tracker reported
three accepted positions, including the final `(192,112)`, and generated a
translated mask preview over frame 2. The new example passed its ctest smoke
gate 1/1; the focused QML control test passed 3/3 after fixing the accepted
point display and disabling redundant acceptance.

## Code-first round cf1 (WP-075 completion)

Written and syntax-checked, not yet built or run; the round build verifies the
tests named below.

1. **Features, coordinate spaces, confidence/failure, mask topology.** Tracks
   are source-pixel points keyed by source frame index (the decoded media
   presentation index, the index `MediaFrameFeed` reports). Point states
   `tracked`/`lost`/`corrected`, track states `idle`/`running`/`ready`/`lost`/
   `cancelled`; a lost point holds its last trusted position with its measured
   confidence. Mask topology rules are unchanged (stable point IDs, linear
   between matching topologies, `heldTopology` otherwise). Docs:
   `docs/tracking-roto.md`, D-143.
2. **Actual tracking, fixtures, correction, worker cancellation.** New
   `TrackingEngine` (`src/core/tracking_engine.*`) runs on a Qt Concurrent
   worker; `cancelAnalysis` is immediate and late samples are dropped by job
   ID; replacing a frame cancels. Subpixel refinement, backward ranges and
   occlusion search (`occlusionFrames`, growing radius, reacquire) added.
   Real footage: `loadMediaFrames` decodes video through the WP-070
   `MediaDecoder` on a worker (`src/core/tracking_roto_media.cpp`), with
   explicit failure for missing media, out-of-range frames and corrupt frames,
   and cancellation. Tests: `tst_tracking_roto_model`
   `subpixelTranslationIsMeasured`, `occlusionHoldsLostFramesAndReacquires`,
   `backwardTrackingRebaseAndReplacedFrames`, `cancellationNeverAppliesPreview`;
   `tst_tracking_media` `decodedFootageTracksAndMasksTheFedFrame` (lossless
   FFV1 clip with a moving feature and two occluded frames),
   `frameLoadingFailsExplicitlyAndCancels`.
3. **Persistent results, offset/rebase, keyframed masks.** Persistence and
   `offsetTrack` existed; `rebaseTrack` re-anchors the reference patch on an
   accepted nonlost point (undoable); `applyTrackToMask` keys a tracked path
   over a range as one undo step and reports `skippedFrames` (lost or
   missing). Tests: `backwardTrackingRebaseAndReplacedFrames`,
   `trackedMaskKeysTrustedFramesAndRendersLayerTime`.
4. **Masks in real composition output; loss exposed.** WP-073's gap is closed
   (D-142): `MotionFrameRenderer` samples a mask at the source frame fed with
   the layer's pixels (`MediaFrameFeed` passes it), else at
   `floor(localTicks / maskTicksPerFrame)`, never composition time.
   `TrackingPanel` gains `occlusionFrames` (sent with `startRequested`) and a
   warning state for reacquired loss. Tests:
   `trackedMaskKeysTrustedFramesAndRendersLayerTime`,
   `decodedFootageTracksAndMasksTheFedFrame` (mask rendered through the feed
   at composition frame 11 = source frame 9), `tst_tracking_roto_controls`
   (occlusion argument).
5. **Verification.** Known transforms (exact integer shifts; smooth
   1.3/0.6 px per frame within 0.2 px, simulated mean 0.05 px), occlusion,
   topology edits (existing `maskInterpolatesRendersAndSurvivesReopen`),
   interrupted analysis and loads, undo of rebase and propagation, and
   rendered masks across frames are covered by the tests above.

Scope notes: rotation/scale/perspective motion models, multi-resolution flow
and camera solving are not part of this package's translation tracker (D-143);
frames above 4 megapixels are refused rather than proxied. Performance
measurement, Qt 6.8 and other platforms, screen-reader runs and translation
are qualification work deferred to WP-088 to WP-094.
