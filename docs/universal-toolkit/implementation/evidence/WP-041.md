# WP-041 — Spatial selection, snapping and transforms

**State: active spatial slice; package not accepted.** WP-040 remains
in review while its WP-033 dependency is open. Luna began the independent visual surface early so Sol can connect the
authoritative geometry and snapping contracts once the canvas dependency is
closed.

`SelectionOverlay` maps four document-space corners, or a rectangular fallback,
through the supplied `CanvasView` pose. It draws a view-space outline with
eight themed handles. Handle drags report document-space start/current points;
keyboard and accessible actions report a nudge request. The component does
not calculate snaps, validate transforms, preview authoritative geometry or
commit document edits. Those are still WP-041 work.

The focused Qt 6.11 QML geometry/drag test passed **3/3**. The preview check
passed; its dump showed `CanvasView#canvasView 0,0 760x520`,
`SelectionOverlay#selectionOverlay 0,0 760x520`, `Item#handleTopLeft 239,189
20x20` and `Item#handleBottomRight 440,271 20x20`. Sol opened the
[compact](WP-041-overlay-compact.png) and
[comfortable](WP-041-overlay-comfortable.png) captures: the frame follows the
rotated shape and the handles remain on corners and edge midpoints.
The refreshed shared Qt 6.11 and Qt 6.8 builds and the same focused 3/3
geometry test passed after integration. `qtk-preview --check` also instantiated
the example from the shared Qt 6.11 build.

`SnapModel` now implements a pure one-point spatial snap query against indexed
visible object geometry, host-supplied guides and grid lines, with deterministic
guide > object > grid priority and zoom-scaled pixel tolerance. The focused
Qt 6.11 and Qt 6.8 focused tests each passed **7/7** (priority,
self-exclusion, zoom tolerance, visibility, stable guide tie-breaking,
disabling mid-gesture and continuous projections onto long and rotated
outline edges). The installed-consumer check is pending. It still needs a
group transform controller and guide persistence.

`GuideLayer` is a host-data visual/request slice. It maps stable guide IDs and
document-space positions through a rotated `CanvasView`, paints clipped lines,
and emits add/move/finish/cancel/delete requests through pointer, keyboard and
accessible actions. Its focused Qt 6.11 and Qt 6.8 QML geometry checks each
passed **4/4**, and the visual fixture passed `qtk-preview --check` on both.
The preview dump places `GuideLayer#guideFixtureLayer 0,0 900x600` under
`CanvasView#guideFixtureCanvas` and exposes
`guideAction_vertical-220` and `guideAction_horizontal-190`. Sol opened the
[compact](WP-041-guides-compact.png) and
[comfortable](WP-041-guides-comfortable.png) captures: two cyan lines follow
the rotated page and cross the document shapes as expected. The visual fixture
is [GuideScene.qml](../../../../examples/spatial-canvas/GuideScene.qml).

`ToolController` now includes a bounded lasso selection tool using the same
indexed polygon hit testing as the marquee. Shift adds, Alt selects only fully
contained objects, and Escape restores the prior selection. The focused
canvas-tool CTest target passed on Qt 6.11.1 and Qt 6.8.3; the Qt 6.8
visual fixture also passed `qtk-preview --check`. The compact dump places
`CanvasView#lassoFixtureCanvas 0,0 900x600` and
`CanvasOverlay#canvasOverlay 0,0 900x600`. Sol opened the
[compact](WP-041-lasso-compact.png) and
[comfortable](WP-041-lasso-comfortable.png) captures: the dashed cyan polygon
surrounds the second object on a rotated page at both densities. The fixture
is [LassoScene.qml](../../../../examples/spatial-canvas/LassoScene.qml).
Selection remains scoped to one controller; a shared authoritative selection
model is still open.

`Tk.SpatialTransform` is an authoritative reference-canvas group move/resize
slice. It captures selected stable objects and a revision, previews without
document mutation, excludes the group from snap targets, and commits one
`CanvasEdit` on finish. The focused `tst_canvas_transform` CTest target passed
on Qt 6.11.1 and Qt 6.8.3. It covers group move/resize and one-step undo,
negative scale, singular rejection, live snap disable from the raw pointer,
cancel/external edit, a thin polyline, and keyboard motion through a rotated
view pose. The visual fixture passed `qtk-preview --check` on both versions.
The compact dump places `CanvasView#transformFixtureCanvas 0,0 900x600`,
`SelectionOverlay#transformFixtureHandles 0,0 900x600`, and
`Item#handleBottomRight 626,427 20x20`. Sol opened the
[compact](WP-041-transform-compact.png) and
[comfortable](WP-041-transform-comfortable.png) captures: eight handles and
the preview frame follow the rotated page while the two document shapes stay
at their canonical positions. The fixture is
[TransformScene.qml](../../../../examples/spatial-canvas/TransformScene.qml).

The controller now also previews and commits group rotation about the
selection center or a host-supplied document-space pivot. Shift constrains
pointer rotation to 15-degree increments; `rotateBy(degrees)` provides a
numeric/keyboard entry point. `previewCorners` keeps the rotating outline
oriented while the objects remain canonical until finish. The overlay adds
the accessible `handleRotate` with pointer, arrow-key and accessible step
requests. The focused Qt 6.11 and Qt 6.8 transform tests each passed
**9/9**, including pivot, preview geometry, one-step undo and Shift snapping;
the overlay QML suite passed **4/4** on each. The rotation fixture passed
`qtk-preview --check` on both versions, with
`SelectionOverlay#transformFixtureHandles 0,0 900x600` and
`Item#handleRotate 502,148 20x20` in the dump. Sol opened the
[compact](WP-041-rotation-compact.png) and
[comfortable](WP-041-rotation-comfortable.png) captures: the cyan preview
frame turns on the rotated page, with the eight resize handles and separate
rotation handle visible while the document shapes remain unmoved. The scene
is [RotationScene.qml](../../../../examples/spatial-canvas/RotationScene.qml).

`CanvasSelectionModel` now owns one stable selected-ID list for multiple
`ToolController` instances over the same document. Tool gestures publish
selection changes; other controllers receive them without a feedback loop.
Deleted, hidden and locked objects are pruned on document edits. The
`spatial-canvas` example binds its two views through this model. Focused
`tst_canvas_selection_model` passed **1/1**, and the example passed
`qtk-preview --check` at 1280×800 on Qt 6.11.

**Still required:** richer angular
snapping and constraints, persistent guide editing,
numeric inspector wiring and pivot persistence, shared-session undo, and dual-Qt
integrated/installed-consumer checks. The reference group move/resize slice
already commits one edit and supports cancel/undo, as does rotation.

The shared Qt 6.11 CTest run passed **102/102** after these slices. The Qt 6.8
run passed **101/102**; its only failure was the existing offscreen RHI
pixel case recorded under [WP-040](WP-040.md#shared-regression-check--later-2026-09-23).
After the item-readback repair and continuous edge projection, both full
integrated Qt suites passed **102/102**; the focused snap suite passed **7/7**
on each version. The earlier failure remains documented above.
