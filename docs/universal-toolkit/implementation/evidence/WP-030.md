# WP-030 — General chart framework

**State: review.** Proposal scope: DAT-10 ChartView, DAT-11 ChartAxis,
DAT-12 ChartSeries, DAT-13 ChartLegend and DAT-15 Histogram, plus VIS-01–03.
The Sol data/axis foundation and Luna visual adapter are copied into the
integration branch. [Visual, export and renderer evidence](WP-030-visual.md)
records the additional focused checks. The package is not accepted.

## Foundation delivered and checked

- Typed `ChartAxis` and `ChartSeries` support numeric, category, logarithmic
  and exact signed-64-bit UTC time domains; stable series/point IDs; declared
  units, gaps and baselines. `ChartData` projects line, step, area, stack,
  bar, waterfall, scatter, bubble and vector geometry, and offers hit lookup,
  decimation and full-resolution table/CSV data. `ChartHistogram` uses
  explicit bin edges and count, probability or density normalization.
- The isolated full build completed at configured parallelism. Focused
  `tst_chart_data` and existing `tst_telemetry` passed **2/2**. Known-answer
  cases cover exact UTC coordinates past 2⁵³, positive/negative stacking,
  grouped bars, waterfall totals, point hit IDs, extrema-preserving
  decimation, histogram edges/densities and CSV quoting. Existing `Graph`
  was not changed.
