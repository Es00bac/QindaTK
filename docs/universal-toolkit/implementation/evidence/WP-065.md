# WP-065 — Take comping

**State: active; package not accepted.**

Luna's `work/wp065-take-comp-view` lane was integrated as `b26694f`.
`TakeCompView` shows the active comp and source take lanes with overlapping
segments, fade edges and exact decimal sample boundaries. Swipe, range,
audition and comparison gestures emit requests; the host supplies all accepted
comp and playback state.

The merged focused QML run passed 4/4, including exact sample ranges beyond
JavaScript's safe integer limit and rejection of invalid snapshots. The
preview dump placed the view at 1056×234, its active comp at 1048×28, and
three take lanes at 1048×55. I opened and inspected the
[merged take-comp capture](WP-065-take-comp.png).

Actual source selection and rendering, crossfade audio, persistence/reopen,
undo, missing-source relink and audition playback remain backend work.

## Code-first lane `work/wp065-cf` (backend; built and run after the audit)

Contract: [audio-comping.md](../../../audio-comping.md); decisions D-174 to
D-177 in [decisions.md](../../../decisions.md). Built on WP-060 (engine,
session), WP-062 (mixer path) and WP-063 (edited source buffers).

1. **Takes, offsets, ranges, overlap/crossfade policy, lifetime** — built.
   `AudioTakeComp` (`audio_take_comp.h/.cpp`): takes are a resource, usable
   frames and a signed source offset at comp sample 0; segments are sorted,
   disjoint and inside their take's coverage; junction crossfades follow
   D-175 (centred, moved to the side that has audio, bounded by half of each
   take's run of touching segments); same-take junctions, gaps and edges cut exactly. Lifetime: take
   media are ordinary session resources; a comp stays in the model when
   timeline undo removes its clip (redo finds it); unavailable media play
   silence and are reported.
2. **Selection/split/merge and playback/render resolution** — built.
   `AudioTakeCompModel` (`session.comps`): `select`, `split`, `merge`,
   `clear`, `setFade`, `preview`/`commitPreview`/`cancelPreview`.
   `AudioRenderGraph::addComp` (`audio_graph_comp.cpp`) plays each resolved
   piece as a graph clip with a frame limit and per-sample fade weights, so
   the device and `renderToFile` share one path; no allocation or tables on
   the device thread. Take audio is never rewritten.
3. **Lane presentation, swipe/numeric/keyboard, comparison/audition** —
   built. `viewState(compId, columns, peaks)` feeds `TakeCompView`
   (boundaries fixed while a preview runs, peaks, availability); numeric
   selection is `select()` with exact decimal strings; the view gains keys
   (cells, Shift extend, take choice, Enter/Space/C), signed offsets,
   `selectionRequested` and `compProposalPreviewCanceled`; `setAudition`,
   `compare` and `toggleComparison` change what the device plays over the
   requested range, and the view can end an audition.
4. **Persistence, undo/redo, missing-source relink** — built. Session part
   `comps` (all or nothing with the other parts), per-owner comp history of
   whole comps, `missingMedia` lists missing take media, `relinkMedia`
   restores them.
5. **Verification** — built and run in `build/cf` (see the audit section):
   - `tst_audio_take_comp` (new): `compEditsAndCrossfadePolicy` (overlapping
     and adjacent selections, ID stability, refusals, exact fade regions,
     moved and cut fades, codec); `loopPassesBecomeTakesOfOneComp` (pass
     offsets from jumps + latency, comp clip placement, 240-sample fade
     pieces); `renderedCompHasExactBoundariesAndUndoes` (every sample of a
     4-sample linear crossfade render, overlap/adjacent boundary samples,
     merge and split render identically, 5 undos back to the recorded
     samples, redo); `previewsAuditionAndComparison` (cancelled and
     committed previews, audition/compare renders);
     `reopenedAndRelinkedCompRendersTheSame` (reopen equivalence, missing
     take renders silence, relink restores); `punchIntoAClipThenIntoItsComp`
     (clip→comp switch with exact punch samples, timeline undo/redo, a
     second punch as one comp step and its undo);
     `takeCompViewDrivesTheComp` (swipe preview/accept, Escape-cancel and
     reject leave samples, audition from the view).
   - `tst_audio_take_comp_engine` (new, silent loopback): wrap frames
     9000 + 8000k, one take per pass, the comp renders the source exactly
     from 1000 to 10000, a punched second recording joins the comp inside
     [3000, 5000), zero device-thread allocations while recording and while
     playing crossfaded comps.
   - `tst_qml -input tests/qml/tst_take_comp_view.qml`: signed offsets,
     keyboard selection, cancelled swipe signal.

**Accept when** — covered by the renders above: comp edits select source
frames by exact offsets, and every edit is reversible (comp undo, timeline
undo for placement, cancel for previews) with identical samples.

Also in this lane: per-pass loop takes and punch recording left by WP-060

## Audit of `9720e36` and gap fixes

An independent read-only audit found five gaps. I re-checked each one
against the plan (`phase-05-audio.md#wp-065`) and the code. All five were
real.

1. **Nothing had been built or run.** The lane now builds `libqindatk`,
   the three comp test binaries, `tst_qml` and `qtk-preview` in `build/cf`
   after the `round/cf1` merge. Results: `tst_audio_take_comp` 9/9,
   `tst_audio_take_comp_engine` 3/3, `tst_audio_take_comp_edges` (new)
   6/6, `tst_qml -input tests/qml/tst_take_comp_view.qml` 8/8.
   `previewsAuditionAndComparison` now listens to previews and auditions
   on the device graph (`CompFixture::device`), because exports no longer
   hear them (see 4).
2. **A split beside a long crossfade moved the fade.** `resolve()` bounded
   each fade side by half of the neighbouring *segment*. The bound now uses
   half of the take's *run* (the touching segments of one take on that side
   of the junction), and every piece of the run carries the run's fade
   regions. A split or merge inside a take therefore never changes a
   sample. `viewState` draws only the part of a fade inside each segment.
   D-175 and `audio-comping.md` say "run".
   Test: `splitsAndMergesBesideALongFadeAreSilent` (240-sample fade: T2
   fades over 910–1150; splits at 1120 and 1090, the merges back and a
   split at 210 all render the same as before).
3. **Audio recorded past the end of a comp (or before sample 0) could not
   be reached.** A recording over a comp clip that reaches outside the comp
   now grows it. The clip switches, in one timeline step, to a grown copy
   with a new ID and shifted positions. It keeps its placement, and audio
   past an edge the clip reaches is selected, so trimming that edge out
   plays it. Timeline undo returns the old comp unchanged. D-177 and
   `audio-comping.md` describe this.
   Tests: `recordingPastTheCompEndGrowsIt` (length 1200 → 1700, trim end
   plays 3000–3399, undo/redo renders equal) and
   `recordingBeforeTheCompStartGrowsIt` (clip source offset 300 samples, samples
   2000–2999 unchanged, trim start plays the pre-roll).
4. **Audition ignored its range, could not be ended from the view, and
   reached exports.** `setAudition`/`compare` take the view's exact
   `[start, end)` range, and `auditionRange()` reads it back.
   `playback(id, committed)` gives offline graphs (`renderToFile`, exports)
   the committed comp only, so previews and auditions are device-only
   (D-176). `TakeCompView` gains `auditionTakeId`, `stopAudition()` and
   `auditionStopRequested`: the auditioned take's lane button reads Stop,
   Space on it or Escape outside a swipe ends the audition.
   Tests: `auditionPlaysItsRangeAndExportsPlayTheComp` (only 2800–2899
   heard, comparison 2100–2199, exports equal the committed comp during an
   audition, a comparison and a preview) and QML
   `test_audition_can_be_stopped`; `takeCompViewDrivesTheComp` checks the
   range and the stop through the real model.
5. **Catalog.** `docs/catalog.json` is regenerated and lists
   `auditionTakeId`, `auditionStopRequested`, `stopAudition`,
   `takeAudition_`, `AudioTakeCompModel` and `auditionRange`.

Each new C++ test fails without its fix: with the run bounds, comp growth
and committed-only offline playback switched off, `tst_audio_take_comp_edges`
failed 4 of its 4 cases. The new edges test uses the new audition API, so it
does not build against `9720e36` at all.

Screens: `qtk-preview examples/take-comp/Main.qml --dump` places
`takeAudition_take-blue-07` at `936,15 42x24 "Stop"` beside `Audition`
buttons at 63×24. [Compact](WP-065-take-comp-compact.png) and
