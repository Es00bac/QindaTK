# WP-052 — Equations, references and document review

**State: active; package not accepted.** WP-051 is still active.

Luna's isolated `work/wp052-panels` lane was integrated as `aab57af`.
It adds `EquationEditor`, `ReferenceManager`, and `ReviewPane`, plus a
host-owned QML fixture. Their requests preserve stable IDs and show broken
references, unsupported equation symbols and orphaned comments. The isolated
`-j24 -l24` build, focused ReferenceManager interaction test (3 passed) and
fixture preview check passed. Sol inspected the [document review screenshot](../../../screenshots/wp052-document-review.png): the three controls and their
error/action states are visible and readable.

Equation AST parsing/layout, reference resolution/formatting, and persistent
review anchors, accept/reject operations and undo remain to be implemented in
the structured document backend. Those missing operations prevent acceptance.

**Accept when.** Equations are inline objects that lay out, paginate and
paint on the page. Field results and review marks are document text and
formats, and every request is one reversible transaction. The
[document review example](../../../../examples/document-review/Main.qml)
now binds the three panels to one `RichTextDocument` and its page view.

Scope notes:

- Footnotes and endnotes need a page-foot note area in pagination. No
  package in the plan owns them, so the integrator should assign them.
  Until then, `insertReference("footnote", …)` is refused as `unsupported`.
- Tracked formatting changes and tracked paragraph marks (split or join)
  are not modelled. Tracking covers characters, objects and fields.
- Output-fidelity comparison of equations with a reference renderer,
  screen-reader runs, translation and performance measurement at large
  field or comment counts belong to WP-088 to WP-094.
