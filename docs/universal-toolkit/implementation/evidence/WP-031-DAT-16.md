## Boundary and remaining work

DAT-16 here accepts raw finite values and computes summary and KDE in the
toolkit. Confidence/credible interval endpoints remain host-supplied, with
their stated meaning; the toolkit never infers them. CSV summary export is
available; image/vector export and a summary-only external statistics provider
are not implemented in this lane. The rest of WP-031 and integration gates
remain with Sol.
