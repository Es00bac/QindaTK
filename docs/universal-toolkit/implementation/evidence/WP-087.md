**Accept when.** The views explain real relationships: a real project scan
gives edges with lines, deleted files and removed references show up after
rescan, and relink candidates and plans come from the project tree. Traces
from Chrome-format files or the index's own export keep exact nanosecond
identity across clock domains. All scan and trace work is bounded and
cancellable.

Scope notes: QML dynamic expressions stay unresolved. Documents are indexed
through their resource links (`indexDocumentResources`), not by parsing each
file format. Kernel/process
profiler collection, Perfetto protobuf and statistical clock alignment are not
included. Performance measurement on large real projects, screen-reader runs
and other platforms belong to WP-088 to WP-094.
