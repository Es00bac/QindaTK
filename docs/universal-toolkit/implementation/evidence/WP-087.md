**Accept when.** The views explain real relationships: a real project scan
gives edges with lines, deleted files and removed references show up after
rescan, and relink candidates and plans come from the project tree. Traces
from Chrome-format files or the index's own export keep exact nanosecond
identity across clock domains. All scan and trace work is bounded and
cancellable.

Scope notes: dependency navigation is list-based (inputs, dependents,
impact) rather than a node-link canvas. Other source formats commit edges
through `commitScanEdges`, and QML dynamic expressions stay unresolved.
Kernel/process profiler collection, Perfetto protobuf and statistical clock
alignment are not included. Performance measurement on large real projects,
screen-reader runs and other platforms belong to WP-088 to WP-094.
