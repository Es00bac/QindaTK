It adds `ChannelStrip`, `MixerView`, `PanningControl`, and `RoutingMatrix` with

## Code-first round (`work/wp062-cf`, on `work/wp061-cf` + `round/cf1`): a real mixer graph

Written and syntax-checked without building. Every test below is new or
extended and has not run yet; the round build runs it. Design:
[audio-mixer.md](../../../audio-mixer.md), decisions D-110 to D-113 in
[decisions.md](../../../decisions.md). The mock-channel fixture is gone:
`examples/mixer-routing.qml` now binds the views to a real `AudioSession` mix.

1. **Identities, layouts, gain/pan laws, sends, mute/solo groups and routing
   constraints.** Channels are `track:<uuid>`, `bus:<uuid>` and `master`;
   parameters are `<channel>/gain|pan|width` and `<send>/level`; ports are
   `main` and `device:<n>[-<m>]@<device>` (`AudioMixer`, `audio_mixer_types.h`).
   Layouts are Mono and Stereo, and the master is always Stereo. Gains are in dB
   with the range minimum as silence (D-101). There are four pan laws (`-3dB`
   constant power by default, `-4.5dB`, `-6dB`, `0dB`) plus stereo width and
   balance (D-111). Sends are pre- or post-fader and follow the channel
   panner. Groups apply their mute or solo to every member. Solo is in place
   (upstream and downstream through outputs and sends), with solo safe. The
   bus graph is acyclic, and ports never fall back (D-112). Tests:
   `tst_audio_mixer::identitiesDefaultsAndParameters`, `::refusedRoutesAreExplained`,
   `tst_audio_mixer_dsp::panLawsPlaceAMonoSource`, `::stereoWidthAndBalance`,
   `::busesSendsAndFadersSum`, `::muteSoloGroupsAndSoloSafe`.
2. **Persistent mixer and routing operations with safe reconfiguration and a
   cycle policy.** `AudioMixerModel` (`session.mixer`) validates each operation
   against the whole mix and records it as one step of its own history (undo
   and redo). Track mute, solo, arm and name stay on the `TimelineTrack`
   (D-110). A route or send that would close a loop is refused with the loop
   spelled out. The session saves the mix as its `mixer` part and loads it
   all or nothing (a looped file is refused whole). Each change recompiles
   `AudioRenderGraph`, which mixes channels in topological order from
   preallocated work memory, and the engine adopts it at the next period
   boundary. Tests: `tst_audio_mixer::undoRedoAndReopen`,
   `tst_audio_mixer_engine::routeChangesDuringPlaybackSwitchAtPeriodBoundaries`
   (loopback take: every sample is the source or exact silence, and both
   switches fall on period boundaries), `::channelMetersMeasurePostFaderLevelsWithoutAllocating`
   (zero device-thread allocations while routes, mutes and faders change).
3. **Virtualized strips and routing matrix with stable selection and
   keyboard/numeric access.** `MixerView` is a recycled `ListView` over
   `AudioMixerStripModel`, whose rows move instead of resetting. It has banks
   of one view width, compact and detail strips, a group bar, selection by
   channel ID, and Left/Right, Home/End and Page Up/Down keys. `RoutingMatrix`
   is bound to the mixer, with keyboard row navigation and explained refusals.
   Numeric access comes from the Fader field and the `PanningControl` percent
   fields. The new `ChannelRoutingPanel` provides the output selector and the
   sends. Tests: `tst_mixer_routing.qml::test_mixer_view_virtualizes_and_keeps_gestures`
   (fewer than 20 strips for 41 channels; arrow selection by ID),
   `::test_bound_routing_explains_refusals`, `::test_panning_positions_are_canonical`.
4. **Automation, meters and undo without delegates as engine state.** Gain,
   pan, width and send levels are `AutomationModel` parameters behind one shared,
   mixer-owned `AudioParameterBinding` each, and a pan/width pad drag is one
   undo step (`commitWith`). Channel meters are post-fader and measured in
   `render()` into a preallocated `AudioChannelMeterSink`. Its frames carry the
   graph generation and drain into `AudioEngine.channelMeter(id)` (D-113).
   `Fader` and `Knob` keep a gesture on the binding it began on, so a
   recycled delegate cannot commit into another channel. While a gesture holds
   a channel, `MixerView` defers bank changes and keeps the held strip, even a
   hidden one, through a reorder. Tests:
   `tst_audio_mixer_dsp::automationDrivesPanAndSendLevels`, `::padGestureIsOneUndoStep`,
   `tst_audio_mixer::stripRowsMoveAndHideWithoutResets`,
   `tst_audio_mixer_engine::channelMetersMeasurePostFaderLevelsWithoutAllocating`,
   `tst_mixer_routing.qml::test_bound_strip_edits_the_engine_parameter`.
5. **Verification.** Known panning and mix output (exact values), mute, solo
   and group interactions, refused feedback routes, route changes during
   playback, and session reopen all run in the tests above. A device port
   stays unchanged, silent and explained after its device goes, while an
   offline render stays the master mix (`tst_audio_mixer::devicePortsNeverFallBack`,
   `tst_audio_mixer_engine::devicePortsNeverFallBackToAnotherDevice`). The
   existing `tst_audio_engine` and `tst_audio_parameter_binding` expectations
   are unchanged: the default mix is bit-exact with the pre-mixer engine.
   The allocation probe moved to `tests/cpp/audio_allocation_probe.h`.

**Accept when** (not yet verified; round build pending): the mixer and routing
UI changes a real audio graph (`tst_mixer_routing.qml` bound tests), and the
graph produces verified output (`tst_audio_mixer_dsp`, `tst_audio_mixer_engine`).
The UI stays responsive because compile runs on the GUI thread, the device
thread only swaps graphs, and the view creates only visible strips; measuring
that responsiveness belongs to WP-088…WP-094.

### Round cf1 build: fixes

The round build (Qt 6.11 and 6.8) reported two `tst_qml` failures and a
visual defect. All three are fixed, and the targets were rebuilt and run in
`build/cf` (offscreen, software). `tst_audio_mixer` 7/7,
`tst_audio_mixer_dsp` 8/8, `tst_audio_mixer_engine` 5/5 and
`tst_qml -input tests/qml/tst_mixer_routing.qml` 7/7 pass with no warnings.

- `test_mixer_view_virtualizes_and_keeps_gestures` read `ListView.count`
  before the view applied the new rows; it now waits (`tryCompare`).
- `test_panning_positions_are_canonical` exposed a real bug. A `Binding`
  whose `when` turns false restores the target's first value by default. A
  slider press, including an arrow key, set the pan slider back to 0 before
  the step, and a host-mode strip fader could jump the same way. Those
  bindings now use `restoreMode: Binding.RestoreNone`.
- The compact grab cut a fourth strip at the panel edge. A bank is now the
  whole strips that fit, stretched to fill the width, and scrolling snaps to
  strip edges. The grabs also showed the pan pad without height (Tk.Flex
  ignores a plain `height`) and short strips squeezing every part. The pad
  now has an implicit height, and only the fader shrinks, down to a minimum.
  `qtk-preview --dump` at 1280x740: `ListView#mixerChannelList 0,0 763x639`
  holds three `Item#mixerStripCell` of 252 px at x 0, 256 and 511, and each
  `Item#panPad` is 236x56. Compact and comfortable grabs were inspected.
