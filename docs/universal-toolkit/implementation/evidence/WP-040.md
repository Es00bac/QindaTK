# WP-040 — Spatial canvas and rendering boundary

**State: review (integrated; WP-033 dependency open).** This
evidence covers proposal scope CAN-01 CanvasView, CAN-02 ToolController,
CAN-03 HitTestService, PLT-10 RenderSurfaceAdapter and FND-06. The contract
is in [canvas.md](../../../canvas.md); the control entries are in
[controls.md](../../../controls.md#canvasview); the design decision is
[D-044](../../../decisions-application.md).

Branch `work/wp040-canvas-opus`, worktree `QindaTK-wp040-opus`, based on
integration commit `1f14cbb`. Build directory `build/wp040` (Debug, Ninja,
default configured parallelism). Qt 6.11.1 on Gentoo Linux; GPU runs used
an AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT (Mesa 26.1.8 radeonsi, OpenGL 4.6).

## What is delivered

- **Transforms.** A pose (`center`, `zoom`, `rotation`) determines
  document→view (and its algebraic inverse) and view→device (DPR).
  Navigation provides cursor-anchored zoom, anchored rotation, pan, and fit
  that respects rotation. Singular and non-finite matrices are detected, and
  a sanitized pose is never singular.
- **Object model.** `CanvasDocument` provides stable 64-bit ids, layers with
  order, visibility and lock, and Rect/Ellipse/Polyline/Image shapes with
  local geometry and a local→document transform. Images are document-owned
  tiled images (256² tiles). The model has a uniform-grid spatial index and
  all-or-nothing `commit` with stale-edit rejection. Damage is reported in
  document coordinates, and history is bounded. Objects with singular
  transforms are kept but neither drawn nor picked.
- **Picking.** `HitTestService` does point and region picking over exact
  geometry, including transformed and sheared shapes. Parts are
  vertex/segment/stroke/fill/image-tile. Tolerance is in logical pixels
  (`px/zoom`). Lock policies are skip/report/block, hidden objects are
  skipped, and ordering is deterministic.
- **Tools.** `ToolController` gives each canvas one gesture at a time, owned
  by the view that began it, with begin/update/end/cancel. A gesture commits
  exactly one edit or none. Cancel reasons: escape, lost-grab, tool-switch,
  document-switch, document-reset, document-changed, provider-destroyed,
  view-detached, navigation-override. Temporary tools are supported. The
  built-in tools are select (click, shift, move, marquee), pan,
  rectangle/ellipse, and pen (with pressure and eraser).
- **CanvasView.** `Tk.CanvasView` over the C++ `CanvasSurface` handles mouse,
  pen, touch (tool, or two-finger pan/pinch/rotate), wheel, trackpad pixel
  deltas, Ctrl/Alt wheel, native zoom/rotate/pan gestures and keyboard
  navigation. Views sharing a `CanvasViewState` stay synchronized. The view
  provides an overlay slot, a focus ring and accessible Canvas role and
  name. `Tk.Viewport` is unchanged.
- **Rendering.** `RenderSurfaceAdapter` tiles in document space at a
  power-of-two LOD. It plans visible tiles against the rotated view quad,
  applies damage invalidation across LODs, a per-frame budget and a
  byte-bounded LRU cache. It carries revision, DPR and colour context
  explicitly, and supports backend selection with fallback and resource
  generations. There are two providers: software (QPainter tiles) and
  scene graph (vertex-coloured geometry and image-tile textures, clipped per
  tile).

### Integration-readiness follow-up

Review against integration `c5a16fe` found and fixed three boundary defects:

- A secondary mouse-button release could end the primary drag or middle-button
  pan. The initiating button now owns release; detaching a tool or view cancels
  an active pointer. `secondaryButtonReleaseKeepsPrimaryGesture` covers both
  cases.
- A finite pose at the signed tile-key limit could overflow conversion or
  loop increment. Visible keys are clipped before conversion and traversed
  with 64-bit counters. `tst_canvas_tile_limits` checks both key limits and a
  wholly out-of-range view.
- A finite, very wide spatial bound could overflow `QRect::width()` and walk
  billions of grid cells. The index now counts cell spans in 64-bit integers;
  `tst_canvas_core` checks a 2e13-unit region against a bounded visit count.

The follow-up also gives the root `Tk.CanvasView` a stable `canvasView`
objectName and renumbers its decision to D-044 after EventLog D-042 and Diff
D-043. `git diff --check` and the changed-file SPDX/500-nonblank-line checks
pass. `cmake -S . -B build/wp040 -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Debug` and
`cmake --build build/wp040 --target tst_canvas_core tst_canvas_render
tst_canvas_tile_limits tst_canvas_input tst_qml` exited 0 with no warnings.
The focused CTest run for those four C++ suites passed **4/4** (2.13 s), and
`tst_canvas_view.qml` passed **6/6** (98 ms) under the software backend.

- The visible-tile bound at 1280×800 is 169 tiles at DPR 1 and 625 at DPR 2
  (`(ceil(2·diag·dpr/256)+1)²`). No frame renders more than 24 tiles, and a
  repeat frame at the same pose renders 0.
- Moving one of 2,500 objects re-rendered **1 of 25** visible tiles; the
  rest kept their serials.
- In the 10k fixture, 200 point probes examined **275** index entries in
  total (273 tested).
- With a 1 MiB limit while panning, the cache stays within
  `max(limit, visible × 256 KiB)` and evicts.

Inspection found two defects, both fixed and guarded:

1. Under the software renderer, a view not at the window origin drew its
   tiles at x = 0, because subtrees added under plain `QSGNode`s lost the
   item transform. The container nodes are now identity `QSGTransformNode`s.
   `synchronizedViews` covers this.
2. Far zoom-out on the GPU showed streaks and missing shapes, because tile
   runs exceeded 65,535 vertices and the batch renderer wrapped its 16-bit
   indices. Runs are now split per geometry node.

## Unsupported or unproven platform cases

- **Real device loss.** CI cannot force a GPU device loss (a D3D TDR,
  `VK_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST` or a GL context reset). Recovery is proven through
  three paths:
  - the `simulateContextLoss(dropCaches)` lifecycle injection;
  - a real scene-graph teardown, by reparenting to a new window and
    destroying the old one;
  - a backend switch.

  Qt's `sceneGraphInvalidated` and `releaseResources` hooks bump the same
  resource generation, but no real device-lost event has been observed.
- **Graphics APIs.** Only OpenGL (threaded loop, AMD/Mesa) and the software
  adaptation were run. Vulkan, Metal and D3D11/12 use the same
  QSGGeometryNode/QSGImageNode path and are untested. Windows and macOS are
  untested.
- **Mixed-DPR displays.** DPR 1/1.25/1.5/2 is covered by planning and
  picking tests and by `devicePixelRatioOverride`. Moving a window between
  real monitors of different scale only re-plans on `screenChanged`; that is
  unverified on hardware.
- **Pen and platform gestures.** Pressure and eraser are verified through the
  tool API. Real tablet delivery relies on Qt's tablet-to-mouse synthesis
  carrying pressure and pointer type. Native gestures are verified by
  sending the events to the item, not through a platform plugin.
- **Colour.** Only the sRGB → sRGB context is implemented. Profile
  transforms are WP-043.

## Acceptance still open

- **WP-033.** There is no session, persistence, recovery or shared-undo
  binding for spatial documents. The `CanvasDocumentAdapter` seam is ready,
  but "a real spatial document … through explicit model/render boundaries"
  is met only in the in-memory reference model.
- **WP-041.** Snapping, selection handles, transform handles, guides and
  keyboard/inspector-equivalent transforms are not implemented. The published
  contracts are `CanvasToolPreview`, `ToolController.selection`,
  `HitTestService`, `mapFromDocument` and `canvasOverlaySlot`.
- Worker-thread tile rendering, mipmapped far-zoom textures and cross-LOD
  placeholder tiles are not implemented. A missing tile shows the canvas
  colour for one refinement pass.
- The Qt 6.8 minimum, installed-consumer check and non-Linux runs remain
  Sol's integration gates. `plan.json` and the execution ledger were left
  for Sol to update. The integration-readiness review renumbered the canvas
  decision to D-044 after EventLog and Diff claimed D-042 and D-043.
