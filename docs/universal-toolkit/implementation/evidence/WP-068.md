# WP-068 — Pitch analysis and correction editing

**State: accepted after integration.**

Luna's `work/wp068-pitch-editor` implementation was integrated from `29e5fbc`.
`PitchEditor` renders a bounded host-supplied pitch contour, note regions and
confidence, then issues pitch, time and formant preview/commit requests with
exact sample strings. The host owns limits, parameter state and edit outcomes.

The isolated build used configured `-j24 -l24`; its focused QML checks passed
2/2 and its preview fixture instantiated. The merged focused QML run passed
4/4 and `example_pitch-editor` passed. The merged dump places
`PitchEditor#pitchEditor` at `0,0 1096x560` and its canvas at `2,2 1092x556`.
That first 1120×720 screenshot showed the host-less fixture; it was replaced
by the round-build screenshots below once the example drove the real host.

## Audit gap closure (unvoiced material, host time and Flatten requests)

An independent audit of `8d37317` found two untested paths. Both are now
covered, and the first one exposed a defect, which is fixed.

**Unvoiced material inside a corrected region (req. 5).** Auditor's probe:
200 Hz voice, 200 ms of noise, voice again, ±300 cents. The voiced interiors
were on target, but the noise lost 31–33% of its energy, and the onset after
the noise kept its original pitch for ~15–20 ms. Causes found:
(a) each unvoiced grain read the input around the nearest hop mark, but
the synthesis marks drift off the hop grid after a shifted voiced run, so
overlapping noise grains read at different offsets and were averaged;
(b) YIN frames whose ±17 ms window reads noise are unvoiced, so the start of
the voice was copied unshifted. Fixes in `src/core/audio_pitch_correct.cpp`
(D-195): unvoiced grains read at the output sample plus a whole-sample offset
held through the run (0 without a time edit; re-synced to the time map past
half a hop under one). Unvoiced frames next to a voiced run join it for
marking while the period before and after their centre repeat
(periodicity ≥ 0.8), up to one frame reach. The same averaging also cost
retimed noise ~40% of its energy (0.61, measured). It now keeps
0.90–0.96.

- `tst_audio_pitch::unvoicedGapsInsideACorrection`: noise and silence gaps ×
  shifts ±100, ±300, ±699. Voiced interiors within 0.25 cents. Gap samples
  25 ms clear of the voice equal the input within 1e-6 (noise energy ratio
  1.000, was 0.674–0.712). Short-window (±9.2 ms) frames within 0.25 cents
  up to 11 ms before the gap and from 21 ms after the onset, within 3 cents
  over the last 11 ms, and within 6 cents from 11 ms after the onset. Edge
  steps ≤ 1.1× the input's.
- `tst_audio_pitch::retimedNoiseKeepsItsLevel`: three stretches, energy
  0.85…1.05× (measured 0.90–0.96).
- The measured join behaviour, the splices in retimed unvoiced runs and their
  energy are listed under the limitations in docs/audio-pitch.md and in D-196.

**Host time and Flatten requests (req. 3).** New `tst_audio_pitch_requests`.
The session fixtures moved to `tests/cpp/audio_pitch_fixture.h`, which keeps
both files under 500 lines.

- `retimingThroughTheHost`: `editFor("time")` on E4 (`note-24600`). Refused:
  an anchor other than the note start ("moving its start") and non-integer
  samples. A target equal to the anchor is unchanged. Edit map: region
  `[1320, 47880)` (the A3 start, at most one E4 length back), anchor 24600,
  target 26000, and no pitch key. The target clamps to 32360 (from 40000) and
  16840 (from 5000). The first note reaches back to 0 and clamps to 900 at
  1.5×. `commitEdit` makes one undo step, "Move sample 24600 to 26000". The
  analysis is stale at once and refuses requests; after re-analysis there are
  two notes, E4 starts within 2 hops of 26000, A3 still starts at 600, and
  both pitches are within 0.05 cents. The render is bit-identical outside
  `[1320, 47880)`. A3 frames are within 0.05 cents; E4 frames within 0.25
  cents (measured 0.08). Undo renders the original, and the re-analysed E4
  start is back at 24600. Redo renders the edit again.
- `flattenThroughTheHost`: ±30 cents of 5 Hz vibrato (worst frame > 25
  cents). With `correctionAmount` 0, a request at the note's own pitch is
  unchanged. With `correctionAmount` 1, it is an edit (`correctionAmount` 1,
  target 5700). The commit is one step, "Correct pitch to A3 (100%)", and the
  stored edit keeps amount 1. Rendered frames are within 4 cents of A3, and
  the re-analysed note is within 1 cent. Undo renders the input.
- Not covered: the "This note has no room to move" branch. With positive
  room on both sides, the clamp interval is never empty (2/3 ≤ 1.5 on both
  sides), and a note would have to start exactly at its region's left edge.
  The analysis never places a note start at sample 0 or on the previous
  phrase's end, so no fixture can reach it. It stays as a defensive guard.
